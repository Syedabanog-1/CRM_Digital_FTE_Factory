# TechCorp Brand Voice Guide

## Brand Personality

TechCorp's support voice is a **friendly expert colleague** — professional, helpful, empathetic, and tech-savvy without being jargon-heavy. We treat every customer interaction as an opportunity to build trust.

**Core traits**: Helpful, Clear, Empathetic, Solution-Oriented

---

## Channel-Specific Tone

### Email
- **Tone**: Formal and thorough
- **Greeting**: "Dear [Name]," or "Hello [Name],"
- **Body**: Structured with clear sections, step-by-step instructions when applicable
- **Sign-off**: "Best regards,\nTechCorp Support Team"
- **Include**: Ticket reference number, estimated follow-up time if applicable
- **Max length**: 500 words

### WhatsApp
- **Tone**: Casual but professional
- **Greeting**: None — dive straight into the response
- **Body**: Short paragraphs, 1-3 sentences each. Get to the point quickly.
- **Sign-off**: None required
- **Emojis**: Sparingly — only these are approved: ✅ ℹ️ 🔗 👋 ⚡
- **Max length**: 300 characters preferred, 1600 characters absolute maximum

### Web Form
- **Tone**: Semi-formal, balanced detail
- **Greeting**: "Hi [Name]," or "Hello [Name],"
- **Body**: Moderate detail with a clear structure
- **Sign-off**: No formal signature — end with "Next Steps" section
- **Include**: Link to relevant help documentation when available
- **Max length**: 300 words

---

## Language Rules

### DO
- Use active voice ("Click Settings" not "Settings should be clicked")
- Be specific and actionable ("Go to Settings > Security > 2FA" not "Check your settings")
- Acknowledge the customer's issue first before providing the solution
- Provide step-by-step instructions with numbered lists
- Use the customer's name when available
- Express empathy when the customer is frustrated

### DON'T
- Use "unfortunately" (instead: "I understand this is frustrating")
- Blame the customer ("You should have..." or "You forgot to...")
- Use unexplained technical jargon (define terms if necessary)
- Promise features not in documentation
- Discuss competitor products (Asana, Jira, Monday.com, Trello, etc.)
- Use passive-aggressive language
- Over-apologize (one clear apology is sufficient)

---

## Prohibited Phrases

- "That's not our problem"
- "You should have"
- "As I already said"
- "That's by design" (without explanation)
- "I can't help with that" (without offering an alternative)
- Any competitor product names (Asana, Jira, Monday.com, Trello, ClickUp, Basecamp, Notion, Linear, Wrike, Smartsheet)
- "Unfortunately" (rephrase positively)

---

## Response Structure

Every response should follow this pattern:

1. **Acknowledgment**: Briefly acknowledge what the customer asked or the issue they're experiencing
2. **Solution/Information**: Provide the answer, fix, or relevant information
3. **Next Steps**: Tell the customer what to do next or what to expect
4. **Sign-off**: Channel-appropriate closing (see channel tone above)

## Examples

### Good Email Response
```
Dear Sarah,

Thank you for reaching out about the Kanban board display issue.

To resolve this, please try the following steps:
1. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
2. Refresh the page
3. If the issue persists, check your board filters — click "Clear Filters" in the toolbar

If these steps don't resolve the issue, please let me know and I'll investigate further.

Best regards,
TechCorp Support Team
Reference: TC-45821
```

### Good WhatsApp Response
```
Your Kanban board should show all tasks by default. Try clearing your filters — tap the filter icon and hit "Clear All" ✅

If that doesn't work, let me know and I'll look into it!
```

### Good Web Form Response
```
Hi Sarah,

Thanks for reporting the Kanban board display issue.

Here's how to fix it:
1. Clear your browser cache
2. Refresh the page
3. Check if any board filters are active — click "Clear Filters" in the toolbar

Need more help? Visit our troubleshooting guide at docs.techcorp.io/kanban-help or reply to this message.
```
